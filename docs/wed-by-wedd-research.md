# Wed by Wedd: public business research

Research date: 2026-10-17.
Status: public-source findings and onboarding hypotheses, not confirmed customer needs.

## Recommendation

Lead with helping Brayden organise the preparation around a personalised ceremony,
not with generating more Australian leads. His website describes a North London
business with UK coverage and selected Australian weddings, and a process built
around questionnaires, planning calls, draft revisions and supplier timings. [S1]
Whether he wants administration tools, directory exposure or something else remains
unknown. Ask before proposing a setup or migration.

## Publicly stated business facts

- Brayden Wedd is the celebrant and MC behind Wed by Wedd, based in North London.
  His website advertises wedding ceremonies, MC services, elopements and vow
  renewals, across the UK and for selected weddings in Australia. [S1]
- The offer centres on ceremonies written from scratch, with a conversational,
  personal delivery rather than generic scripts. [S1]
- His published process is an introductory call, questionnaires and a planning
  call, a full draft with revisions, then on-the-day coordination. He supplies
  suppliers with a ceremony plan and timings. [S1]
- Website enquiries collect the couple's names, email, optional phone, wedding
  date, location, service choice and a description of their plans. Pricing depends
  on date, location and whether the booking is ceremony, MC or both. The form's
  backend and any existing CRM integration were not inspected. [S1]
- His public Celebrant Directory profile repeats the North London, bespoke
  ceremony and MC positioning. It describes individual questionnaires for both
  partners and optional contributions from friends and family. This is
  business-authored profile copy, not independent verification. [S2]

## Plausible Wedding Savvy fit

These are hypotheses based on the published workflow, not evidence of pain,
current tool usage, purchase intent or actual product adoption.

| Opportunity | Evidence and proposed starting point |
| --- | --- |
| Questionnaire preparation | Questionnaires are already part of his service. Explore moving one existing questionnaire into Wedding Savvy, without assuming separate private partner responses or family contributions are supported. [S1][S2][S3] |
| Enquiry tracking | His website collects booking enquiries. Explore an enquiry pipeline and, if appropriate, an embedded form; do not assume the current form is disconnected or losing leads. [S1][S3] |
| Preparation reminders | Calls, drafts and event preparation create milestones. Explore tasks and workflow reminders around those milestones, while keeping ceremony writing personal. [S1][S3] |
| Service packages and quotes | Ceremony, MC and combined bookings suggest reusable quote structures with adjustments for location and scope. Service catalogs and quote templates are advertised product features. [S1][S3] |

Wedding Savvy's vendor page advertises questionnaires, embedded forms, workflows,
service catalogs, quote templates and Google Calendar integration. Several are
listed under Pro. These are marketing-page claims, not an end-to-end product test
or confirmation of Brayden's plan or access. [S3]

## Important fit questions

- **International support:** Brayden markets primarily from London, while Wedding
  Savvy's vendor page addresses Australian professionals. Before recommending
  quotes or payments, verify GBP support, UK address/service-area handling,
  Europe/London scheduling including daylight saving, and UK Stripe eligibility
  in the actual product. No incompatibility was established. [S1][S3]
- **Questionnaire privacy:** His process includes individual responses and optional
  family contributions. Verify recipient access, confidentiality and sharing
  behaviour before moving real client stories into the platform. [S2]
- **Existing tools and goals:** His CRM, calendar setup, booking volume, acquisition
  channels, administrative difficulties and reason for joining are unknown. Public
  directory listings show presence, not which channel generates bookings. [S1][S2]
- **Personal service:** Frame automation as support for preparation and follow-up,
  not replacement of his bespoke ceremony writing. This recommendation follows
  his stated positioning; it is not a preference he has expressed to Wedding
  Savvy. [S1]

## Suggested first message

Draft only; not sent:

> Hi Brayden, welcome to Wedding Savvy. I had a look at Wed by Wedd and saw how
> much preparation goes into your personalised ceremonies. What were you hoping
> Wedding Savvy could help with first: new enquiries, questionnaires and planning,

If administration is the priority, offer to map one existing booking process
before suggesting that he migrate tools or enable automation.

## Scope and evidence limits

Public business research only. No private-life research, production database
queries, account logins, contact-form submissions or outreach were performed for
this research. Business claims are attributed to their publishers. Review counts,
credentials, revenue and customer volume were not independently verified. The
website's testimonials are selected marketing material, not a representative
customer sample. [S1]
